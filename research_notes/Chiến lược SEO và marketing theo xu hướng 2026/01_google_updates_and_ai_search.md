## 1. Confirmed Google ranking updates (Dec 2025 → Nov 2026)

### Takeaway
Google ran two broad core updates in 2026 (March, May), one Discover-only core update (February), four spam updates (March, June, August, September) and one new spam policy (back-button hijacking, enforced from 15 Jun 2026). No core update was confirmed between June and 28 Oct 2026, although trackers saw heavy volatility on 20–21 and 24 Oct 2026 (Discover traffic drops reported). Nothing in 2026 introduced a new "AI content" rule: the updates enforce existing policies (scaled content abuse, site reputation abuse, expired domain abuse, malicious practices).

## 2. AI Overviews and AI Mode: reach, Vietnamese availability, product direction

### Takeaway
Both AI surfaces are live in Vietnamese: AI Overviews since late Oct 2024 (100+ country expansion) and AI Mode since 8 Oct 2025. In 2026 Google made AI Mode the natural continuation of AI Overviews ("Show more" hands off into an AI Mode chat, global since Jan 2026 on mobile), moved AI Mode to Gemini 3.5 Flash and launched a redesigned "intelligent" search box and background search agents at I/O (19 May 2026). Google says AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly users. Blue links still appear; Google told Press Gazette the AI Mode handoff is optional, not a default.

## 3. Click loss: zero-click and CTR studies

### Takeaway
Every 2025–2026 dataset shows fewer clicks per search. When an AI Overview appears, organic CTR roughly halves or worse (Ahrefs −58% for position 1; Pew 8% vs 15% of users clicking; Seer AIO-query CTR fell to ~0.6% in mid-2025, then partly rebounded to 2.4% in Feb 2026). SparkToro/Similarweb: 68% of US Google searches ended without a click in Jan–Apr 2026. Publisher organic Google traffic fell about a third in the year to Nov 2025 (Chartbeat via Reuters Institute, secondary). Planning implication: rankings no longer translate linearly into traffic; visibility inside the answer, brand demand and conversion per visit matter more.

### Cited findings
- Seer Interactive v3 (53 brands, 5.47M queries, Jan 2025–Feb 2026): organic CTR on AIO queries rebounded from 1.3% (Dec 2025) to 2.4% (Feb 2026), "rebound 85%"; earlier low ~0.61% (Sep 2025, −61%); non-AIO informational CTR also fell 41% Feb→Sep 2025 — [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/blog/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update), [Seer Sep 2025](https://www.seerinteractive.com/blog/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-september-2025-update)
- Ahrefs (Feb 2026, 300k keywords): AIO presence correlates with 58% lower CTR for the top-ranking page — [summary](https://seoworth.it.com/news/ahrefs-ai-overviews-ctr-study/)
- Pew Research: 8% of users click an organic result when an AIO appears vs 15% without — via [ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/google-ai-overviews-organic-ctr-2026/)
- SparkToro (Similarweb panel): 68.01% of US Google searches Jan–Apr 2026 ended with no click, vs 60.45% in 2024 (panel changed, part of the jump is methodology) — [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/in-2026-less-than-one-third-of-google-searches-still-send-a-click/), [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-reach-68-in-early-2026-study-479717)
- Similarweb 2025: zero-click rose 56% → 69% (May 2025) after AIO launch — [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/similarweb-google-zero-click-search-growth-39706.html)
- Chartbeat via Reuters Institute: publishers' organic Google traffic −33% globally, −38% US, year to Nov 2025 (secondary vendor blog) — [Gist](https://gist.ai/blog/zero-click-crisis-publisher-traffic)

## 4. ChatGPT as a search/ads channel

### Takeaway
ChatGPT has ~900M weekly users (OpenAI, Feb 2026) and launched ads in 2026. Ads reached Vietnam on 23 Sep 2026 (SEA + Taiwan wave, self-serve, Free and Go tiers only). By 21 Oct 2026 ChatGPT Ads ran in 89 countries with 100k+ advertisers. ChatGPT referral traffic is growing fast but is still a small share of total referrals.

### Cited findings
- 900M weekly users and 50M paying subscribers (Feb 2026) — [ROIC](https://www.roic.ai/news/openais-chatgpt-surpasses-900-million-weekly-users-50-million-paying-subscribers-02-27-2026)
- Ads timeline: US pilot 9 Feb 2026 → AU/NZ/JP/KR/IN → 31 European markets 24 Aug 2026 (no personalisation in Europe at launch) → SEA + Taiwan (Indonesia, **Vietnam**, Thailand, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Taiwan) 23 Sep 2026 → +26 countries, 89 total, 21 Oct 2026; 100k+ advertisers; self-serve via Ads Manager; shown only to logged-in adults on Free/Go — [OpenAI SEA](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-southeast-asia-taiwan/), [OpenAI 26 countries](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-26-new-countries/), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/openai-gains-7-asian-markets-for-chatgpt-ads-passing-60-countries/), [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/chatgpt-ads-makes-its-southeast-asia-debut)
- Referral growth: ChatGPT referral traffic +101% Jan→Aug 2026 (BrightEdge, secondary); ChatGPT sends ~95% of AI-assistant clicks but ~0.53% of all referrals (secondary, methodology unclear) — [Rise at Seven](https://riseatseven.com/blog/aeo-ai-search-statistics/), [Stan Ventures](https://www.stanventures.com/news/the-state-of-chat-gpt-july-2026-7429.md.txt)

## 5. Ads inside Google AI surfaces

### Takeaway
Ads now run inside AI Overviews and AI Mode with no separate targeting or opt-out (same auction as Search). Google Marketing Live (20 May 2026) added conversational/AI-native formats; on 28 Oct 2026 Google reportedly extended AI Mode ads to 10 more countries and launched multi-turn ads (US). Single-source details; verify before relying on them.

### Cited findings
