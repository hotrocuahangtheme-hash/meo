# SEO trend outlook: search volume, the business of search, regulation, Vietnam specifics, 2027 predictions

Research date: 2026-11-04. **[Snippet]** level (search summaries; direct fetch blocked). This note adds to notes 01–07 for the standalone SEO trend report.

## 1. Search is growing, but the value of a search is changing

### Takeaway
Alphabet's Q3 2026 results (28 Oct 2026) say search demand is not shrinking. Search & other revenue grew 12% (~US$63.4B), and Google says year-on-year query growth was the highest in five years, driven by AI features. AI Mode queries are ~3× longer than classic queries, 1 in 5 AI Mode searches uses voice, images or documents, and visual searches top 50B a month. The catch: management said AI Mode monetises less than classic search. Search revenue was roughly flat quarter-on-quarter, and the stock fell >10% (also on a free-cash-flow drop). Google's worldwide search share stays around 89–91% (Statcounter 2026), with Bing at ~4–5% (≈10% on desktop). Chatbots are measured separately and are not in these shares.

## 2. The SEO industry is re-tooling around "AI visibility"

### Takeaway
Adobe completed its acquisition of Semrush on 28 Apr 2026 (US$1.87B per Adobe's 10-Q), explicitly to sell "SEO, GEO and agentic search optimisation" inside its CX suite next to Adobe LLM Optimizer. Together with the first-party AI reports of 2026 (Search Console gen-AI report, Bing AI Performance, GA4 AI Assistant channel; see notes 02 and 07), this marks the industry's shift: measurement moves from rank and clicks to citations, mentions and share of voice in AI answers. Vietnamese agencies follow the same script ("SEO and GEO in parallel"). Note: some write-ups use "GEO" to mean "Google Enhanced Overview" rather than "Generative Engine Optimisation", so define terms with vendors.

## 3. Regulation and platform deals that reshape search

### Takeaway
- **US v. Google (search):** Judge Mehta's Sep 2025 remedies did not force a Chrome sale. They do require Google to share parts of its search index and user-interaction data with "qualified competitors" and to offer syndicated results/ads, under declining caps over the remedy period. Google appealed on 16 Jan 2026 and asked to pause data sharing. DOJ and states cross-appealed. Oral argument was expected in late 2026, and the status of the stay was not found. If upheld, rival and AI search engines get better index access.
- **UK CMA:** ordered a publisher control. Google answered with the Search Console AI opt-out (Jun 2026; see note 02).
- **Apple:** the Gemini-powered Siri "answer engine" (planner + knowledge search + summariser on Apple servers) was reported for iOS 26.4/26.5 with fuller features in iOS 27 (Sep 2026). What actually shipped was not confirmed. Google's Safari default deal stayed in place.

## 4. Vietnam-specific search trends

### Takeaway
- Google still has ~95.6% of search in Vietnam (note 06).
- AI assistants are mainstream. Sensor Tower counts 18.6B AI-app sessions in H1 2026 (>2× YoY) and ~700M hours on AI apps in H1 2026. In Q1 2026, ChatGPT and Gemini led monthly users and the Vietnamese app **AI Hay** ranked #3. Reviewers note AI Hay answers Vietnamese policy questions (social insurance, traffic law) well because it draws on Vietnamese sources.
- **Cốc Cốc** runs quarterly search-trend reports. In Q2 2026, AI was a leading search theme (ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Hay, Grok, DeepSeek; ByteDance's Dola AI +2,476%), alongside heavy searches about new government policies. Q1 2026 searches centred on prices, taxes and income. Cốc Cốc offers an AI Search that summarises "reputable sources".
- **Most Vietnamese brands are not visible in AI answers yet.** Kompa's "AI Visibility 2026" index of securities brands found >80% invisible on ChatGPT and Gemini. This is the first such Vietnamese study, for one sector.

## 5. 2027 predictions (industry forecasts; low evidential weight)

### Takeaway
Forecasts agree on direction, not numbers:
- zero-click keeps rising (estimates range 60–75%+ by 2027);
- AI agents increasingly do the searching, so CTR loses meaning as a signal;
- success metrics move to brand mentions, citations and downstream traffic quality;
- thin "what is X" pages lose, deep authoritative and original content wins;
- clear entities, structured data and agent-usable formats (tables, specs, FAQs) matter more.
Treat all percentages as vendor estimates.
