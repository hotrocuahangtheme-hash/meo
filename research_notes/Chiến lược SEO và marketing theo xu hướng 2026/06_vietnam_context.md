# Vietnam context 2026: audiences, platforms, search, AI adoption, and the three new laws

Research date: 2026-11-03. **[Snippet]** level throughout. Legal points come from law-firm summaries (Tilleke & Gibbins, Baker McKenzie, Duane Morris, PwC, Hogan Lovells via JD Supra), not from the official gazette text. Check the official text before acting on any specific obligation.

## 1. Audience and platforms

### Takeaway
Vietnam is mobile-first and social-first, but search is still almost entirely Google. DataReportal (Oct 2025): 85.6M internet users and 79.0M social media user identities. Zalo is the default messaging/CRM layer (≈78–81M users, self-reported by VNG). Facebook reaches ~79–86M depending on the counter, YouTube ~62M, and TikTok ads reached 40.9M adults (2025). Google holds ~95.6% of search (Statcounter, Jun 2026); Cốc Cốc ~3.6% (≈6% on desktop) and Bing ~0.4%. So SEO in Vietnam still means Google SEO, while AI Overviews / AI Mode in Vietnamese (note 01) and ChatGPT (ads since Sep 2026) are the new layers on top.

## 2. E-commerce: a Shopee–TikTok Shop duopoly

### Takeaway
Vietnam's four big marketplaces sold ~$16.3B in 2025 (+34.8%). TikTok Shop went from 29% to 41.3% share in 2025 and ~44% by mid-2026 (Shopee ~53%, Lazada ~3%, Tiki collapsing). Growth is driven by "shoppertainment": short video + livestream + affiliate KOC. In H1 2025 livestream revenue grew 2.3× and short-video revenue 1.9×. Both leaders raised seller fees in H1 2026, prompting a competition-authority review. So owned channels (website + Zalo OA + email) matter more for margin.

## 3. AI adoption

### Takeaway
Vietnamese consumers adopted AI quickly. 78% of the online population had used AI by 2025: ChatGPT 81% of users, Gemini 51%, Meta AI 36% (Decision Lab, n=600). In a small 2026 urban survey (Q&Me, n=200), 62% of buyers used AI to research purchases, "but Google still gets the last word". Microsoft puts Vietnam's working-age AI use at 27.9% (Q2 2026), second in SEA after Singapore. No Vietnamese-language study of which sources AI assistants cite was found.

## 4. Three laws that change marketing operations in 2026

### Takeaway
1. **Personal Data Protection Law (Law 91/2025/QH15)**, effective 1 Jan 2026, with implementing Decree 356/2025/ND-CP. It is consent-centred, with no "legitimate interests" basis, so direct marketing and behavioural advertising need explicit, separate consent. Penalties under Decree 330/2026/ND-CP (Aug 2026): buying/selling personal data up to 10× the revenue gained (floor VND 3B); cross-border transfer violations up to 5% of prior-year (Vietnam) revenue; other violations up to VND 3B. Small businesses and startups get a 5-year grace period unless they process sensitive or large-volume data.
2. **Amended Advertising Law (Law 75/2025/QH15)**, effective 1 Jan 2026. New Art. 15a on "advertising conveyors" (KOL/KOC/influencers): they must verify the advertiser and product information, disclose that content is advertising, and they share legal liability. "Online advertising" explicitly covers social media and digital platforms.
3. **E-commerce Law (Law 122/2025/QH15)**, passed 10 Dec 2025, effective 1 Jul 2026. Livestream sales presenters must verify identity electronically (Art. 22) and provide information to the platform (Art. 24). Platforms must offer warning tools for risky goods, and cross-border platforms get new duties. Implementing decrees were pending as of early 2026.
