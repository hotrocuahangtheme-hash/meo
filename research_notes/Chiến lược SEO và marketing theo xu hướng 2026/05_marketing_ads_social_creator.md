# Marketing trends 2026: AI-run ad platforms, creators, email/inbox AI, privacy & data

Research date: 2026-11-03. **[Snippet]** level throughout. Market-size numbers from vendor surveys are directional only.

## 1. Ad platforms hand more of the work to AI

### Takeaway
All major ad platforms now push "give us the goal, budget and assets; the AI does the rest".
- **Google:** AI Max for Search left beta and became available to all advertisers globally in Apr 2026. Dynamic Search Ads are being migrated into AI Max (auto-upgrade from Sep 2026; one source says the deadline moved to Feb 2027). Ads in AI Overviews/AI Mode are only eligible for Search campaigns with AI Max or broad match, Shopping and Performance Max; exact/phrase-only campaigns are excluded. GML 2026 added conversational/AI-native formats (see note 01).
- **Meta:** Advantage+ is the default buying mode. Zuckerberg's stated end state is "objective + budget in, AI creates, personalises and targets". Meta reports a $60B+ run rate for its end-to-end AI ad solutions and ~10M advertisers using its gen-AI creative tools in a quarter (secondary reporting; quarter not confirmed). A fully automated URL-to-ad product was not confirmed as launched.
- **OpenAI:** ChatGPT Ads launched 2026; in Vietnam since 23 Sep 2026 (see note 01).
- Practitioner caution: automation needs guardrails such as brand guidelines, negative keywords/brand exclusions, and creative review. One analysis of 900 campaigns saw invalid traffic rise from 3.7% to 5% with AI Max on. Independent results are mixed (median +13% conversion value in one 250-campaign analysis vs Google's +14% conversions claim).

## 2. Creator / influencer marketing keeps taking budget

### Takeaway
Creator marketing is the fastest-growing budget line. IAB projects US creator ad spend at $44B in 2026 (from ~$37B in 2025). 57% of ad buyers plan to raise creator spend (48% in 2025). 62–74% of brands surveyed are increasing influencer budgets. Measurement lags: many brands grow spend faster than they fix attribution and incrementality. Creators also feed AI visibility, since YouTube and other video mentions correlate most with being cited (note 02). Meta's "ads with creators" (partnership ads) reportedly reached a ~$15B run-rate.

## 3. The AI inbox changes email

### Takeaway
From Jan 2026, Gmail rolled out Gemini features: AI summaries above messages, an "AI Inbox" that ranks mail by inferred importance, and relevance-sorted Promotions. Summaries hit marketing mail hardest. Open rates are now even less reliable, because automatic summarisation and Apple MPP both inflate opens. Teams should judge email on clicks, conversions and replies, test how Gmail summarises each campaign, and put the key offer and CTA in the first lines.

### Cited findings
- Gmail Gemini rollout Jan 2026; summaries/AI Inbox; opens inflated; shift to clicks/conversions — [Braze](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/gemini-gmail-android-ai), [Validity](https://www.validity.com/blog/whats-really-behind-gmails-open-rate-drop-and-what-to-do-about-it/), [Folderly](https://folderly.com/blog/gmail-gemini-ai-email-deliverability-2026), [Mailpro](https://www.mailpro.com/blog/ai-inbox-problem-email-marketing)

## 4. Cookies and data: the deprecation that never happened

### Takeaway
Chrome kept third-party cookies (the reversal was announced in Apr 2025), and Google retired most Privacy Sandbox APIs (Oct 2025). There is no active deprecation timeline, but signal loss continues through browser privacy features, consent rules and shrinking audience lists. The durable plan is still first-party data (accounts, CRM, Zalo/OA followers, email/SMS opt-ins), server-side tagging, and conversion APIs (Meta CAPI, Google enhanced conversions). In Vietnam, all of this sits under the consent-based PDPL (note 06).
