# Local SEO and e-commerce / agentic commerce (2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-03. **[Snippet]** level throughout.

## 1. Local: Google Business Profile → Gemini-powered Maps

### Takeaway
Local search is being rewritten by Gemini. Google retired Business Profile Q&A (API discontinued 3 Nov 2025, public section phased out from Dec 2025 into early 2026) and replaced it with **Ask Maps**, a Gemini conversational layer in Google Maps. Ask Maps launched in the US and India on 12 Mar 2026 and had expanded to 150+ countries by Aug 2026, including Malaysia and Indonesia in SEA (whether Vietnam is included was not confirmed). It answers from the places database (300M+ places), profile fields (services, attributes, hours, description) and **review text** from 500M+ contributors. Businesses cannot edit or flag its answers. The only lever is better profile data and richer, specific reviews. Google has not said whether placement can be paid for.

## 2. Agentic commerce: what actually happened in 2026

### Takeaway
- **Google went all-in.** The Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP, open standard with Shopify; partners incl. Walmart, Target, Etsy, Wayfair) was announced at NRF on 11 Jan 2026. Checkout inside AI Mode went live in the US on 11 Feb 2026 (Etsy, Wayfair, Chewy, Quince first) via Google Pay/Wallet. Mar 2026 added cart, catalog and identity linking. May 2026 (GML/I-O) added **Universal Cart** across merchants and UCP checkout in YouTube Shopping ads. Also from Jan 2026: **Business Agent**, a branded AI sales associate inside Search (US, Merchant Center + 50 approved offers + claimed brand profile).
- **OpenAI pulled back.** ChatGPT Instant Checkout (Sep 2025, Agentic Commerce Protocol with Stripe) was deprioritised in Mar 2026. Discovery stays in ChatGPT, purchase happens on the merchant's own site. Walmart's in-ChatGPT checkout reportedly converted about 3× worse than click-outs. Product feeds still drive ChatGPT shopping visibility.
- **Product data is the new SEO surface.** Merchant Center added optional "conversational attributes" (Q&A/use-case/suitability fields) and AI performance insights. Google says one Merchant Center feed now powers about 7 surfaces (AI Mode, Gemini shopping, Business Agent, etc.).
- **AI-referred shoppers are high-intent** (Adobe, US retail): AI-referred traffic +393% YoY in Q1 2026. It converted 42% better than non-AI traffic in Mar 2026, 54% in May and 60% in Jul 2026, with 53% more revenue per visit (Jul). It is still a small share of total traffic (<0.2% per one academic last-click study).
- None of the 2026 agentic-checkout launches covered Vietnam (US-first). For Vietnamese merchants the near-term work is feed quality and on-site checkout. Native AI checkout is a watch item.
