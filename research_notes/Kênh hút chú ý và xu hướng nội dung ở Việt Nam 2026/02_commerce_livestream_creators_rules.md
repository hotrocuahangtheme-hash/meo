# Social commerce, livestream, creators, short drama, and the rules around them (Vietnam 2025–2026)

Research date: 2026-11-04. **[Snippet]** level throughout. Many numbers come from agencies (AccessTrade, AnyMind, REVU) or platforms themselves (Shopee, TikTok, Google). They are directional, not audited.

## 1. Shoppertainment: video + livestream is where online buying happens

### Takeaway
Vietnamese e-commerce is now driven by content. In the six weeks before Tết 2026 (15 Dec 2025 – 25 Jan 2026), TikTok Shop sold slightly more than Shopee for the first time (VND 34.6T vs 33.1T of a 67.7T total, Metric). For the full first half of 2026, TikTok Shop held ~44% vs Shopee ~53%. Q1 2026 revenue on the four big marketplaces was VND 148.6T, +46.6% YoY. Livestream accounts for an estimated ~20% of e-commerce sales (AccessTrade, early 2026). The most used livestream-selling platforms are Facebook (31.9%), Shopee (30.9%) and TikTok (17.2%), with ~2.5M selling livestreams a month by 50K+ sellers. Shopee Live + Shopee Video drew ~16B views over Tết 2026.

## 2. Creators: from celebrity KOLs to networks of micro/nano creators and KOCs

### Takeaway
AnyMind's "State of Influence in APAC 2026" puts TikTok at ~63% of influencer-campaign usage in Vietnam, Facebook ~29% and Instagram ~3%. Fashion & Beauty is the biggest spender (~47%). Nano-creators get high engagement (median ~16% on TikTok, ~9% on Facebook). Agencies describe a shift to multi-tier networks (KOL → KOC → creator → seeder → UGC). The 2025 Kera candy scandal hurt trust: Quang Linh Vlogs and Hằng Du Mục publicly apologised in Mar 2025, were fined VND 140M, and were later arrested for "deceiving customers". The amended Advertising Law (effective 1 Jan 2026) now makes endorsers verify products and share liability.

## 3. Formats with momentum

### Takeaway
- **Vertical short drama** is the breakout format of 2025–26. In Vietnam, short-drama apps overtook OTT apps in downloads from Q4 2025 (Sensor Tower), and downloads grew +271% YoY in Q4 2025 (Adjust). By 2026, short-drama apps dominated the App Store's free entertainment chart, led by TikTok's own **PineDrama**, then DramaBox, NetShort, DramaWave… Globally: 850M+ downloads in Q1 2026 (+140%) and ~US$750M in in-app revenue (+20%). Tết 2026 saw a "3-minute drama" craze. Local players (e.g. Yeah1) are producing vertical drama.
- **YouTube Shorts**: uploads by Vietnamese creators more than doubled YoY. New Shorts revenue-share threshold for Vietnamese creators from 1 Feb 2027: 10M eligible Shorts views in 90 days.
- **TV/entertainment on YouTube**: broadcasters are moving shows to YouTube to reach audiences (Saigon Times, Jul 2026). Big music/reality franchises (Anh Trai "Say Hi", Em Xinh "Say Hi", Rap Việt) top YouTube trending.
- **Biggest YouTube channels by subscribers** are kids/family and Shorts channels (Linh Nhi Shorts ~27.6M, Củ Cải ~20M, POPS Kids, BHMEDIA kids, FAPTV, Sơn Tùng M-TP). Snapshots differ by source.
- **Cultural/patriotic content and AI image play** were the defining search themes of 2025 (Google YIS). They are attention magnets for brands that engage authentically.

## 4. Rules that shape the channels

### Takeaway
- **Decree 147/2024/ND-CP** (effective 25 Dec 2024): social accounts must verify with a Vietnamese phone number to post, comment or livestream. Commercial livestream requires personal-ID verification. Under-16s need a guardian.
- **Decree 117/2025/ND-CP** (from 1 Jul 2025): marketplaces with payment functions (Shopee, Lazada, Tiki, TikTok Shop) withhold VAT and PIT for household and individual sellers. This raises the effective cost of marketplace selling and pushes formalisation.
- **E-commerce Law 122/2025/QH15** (effective 1 Jul 2026): livestream sellers must verify identity (Art. 22). **Amended Advertising Law 75/2025/QH15** (1 Jan 2026): endorsers' obligations (Art. 15a). **PDPL** (1 Jan 2026): consent-based marketing (see SEO notes 06).
- Platform fee increases in H1 2026 on Shopee and TikTok Shop led the competition authority to review them.
