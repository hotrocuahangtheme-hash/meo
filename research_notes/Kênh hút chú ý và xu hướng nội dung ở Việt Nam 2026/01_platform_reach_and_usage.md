# Where Vietnamese attention is in 2026: platform reach, time spent, search and AI

Research date: 2026-11-04. Access: direct page fetches are blocked in this session (network policy), so every fact is **[Snippet]** level, taken from search-result excerpts and summaries. Audience numbers from ad tools (DataReportal) count accounts or "ad reach", not unique people. Survey numbers (Q&Me, Decision Lab) are mostly urban samples (Hanoi and HCMC).

## 1. Reach: who is on which platform

### Takeaway
Facebook is still the widest net: about 79M ad audience, +7.2% YoY to Oct 2025. TikTok is now almost as wide (76.1M adults, +9.9% YoY) and is the fastest-growing big platform. YouTube reaches 93% of Vietnamese internet users every day, per Google (Jul 2026). Zalo has ~78–81M users and is the communication/CRM layer. Instagram (+13% YoY) and LinkedIn (10M members, +16%) are small but growing. Threads usage rose from 10% to 23% of surveyed users between Q2 2024 and Q4 2025 (Decision Lab). Decision Lab calls 2025–26 a "stabilisation" phase: total engagement is flat, audiences are more selective, and preferences split more by age (TikTok leads with Gen Z; Facebook/Zalo with older users).

## 2. Time spent

### Takeaway
Vietnamese smartphone users spend about 7 hours a day in apps (Q&Me: 7.3 h in 2025, 7.1 h in 2026, ~27 apps a week). Five apps take ~80% of that time: Facebook, Zalo, TikTok, YouTube, Messenger. TikTok's share of screen time doubled from 11% (2023) to 20% (2025) and it is the #1 app for under-26s. Zalo moved to #2 in time spent overall. TikTok Vietnam says users watch about 100 million hours a day (Jun 2026), with 70M+ monthly and 50M+ daily users.

### Cited findings

## 3. Search and AI assistants

### Takeaway
Google is still where "last word" research happens: ~95.6% search share (Statcounter, Jun 2026, see SEO notes). AI chatbots are now a real channel. In Vietnam ChatGPT holds ~71% of AI-chatbot traffic and Gemini ~21% (Statcounter, Jun 2026); on mobile ChatGPT has ~83%. Google says Vietnam leads Southeast Asia in learning with Gemini. Globally Gemini is catching up (Similarweb: ChatGPT web share fell from ~86% to ~64–65% in a year). There is no reliable 2025–26 Vietnamese survey on Gen Z using TikTok instead of Google. The often-quoted "40%" is Google's 2022 global figure, and Vietnamese research on this is qualitative only (Mibrand focus groups).

## 4. Messaging and Zalo specifics

### Takeaway
Zalo is the default channel for 1:1 and group communication and for customer service (OA, ZNS, Mini Apps). Paid tiers have existed since Aug 2022: free accounts are capped at 1,000 contacts, and Zalo Cloud free storage was cut to 500MB in 2023. In Jan 2026 the National Competition Commission fined VNG (Zalo) VND 810M and TikTok VND 880M for consumer-protection violations around consent to personal-data use. This came just as the Personal Data Protection Law took effect (1 Jan 2026). Meta's 2026 outlook for Vietnam stresses "conversational business": discovery on social and video, AI-assisted consideration, purchase completed in messaging.
