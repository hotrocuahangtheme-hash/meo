# Ad spend and channel economics in Vietnam (what is known, what is not)

Research date: 2026-11-04. **[Snippet]** level. This is the weakest-evidence area of the research: no independent 2026 breakdown of Vietnamese ad spend by platform was found.

## Takeaway
- **Market size is uncertain.** Digital ad market estimates for 2025–26 range from ~US$1.1–1.5B (IMARC, P&S, Ken Research) to ~US$5.4B (Research and Markets, 2026), depending on definitions. Mobile takes ~65% of digital spend (P&S, 2025). Retail media (ads inside Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada…) was ~16% of digital in 2025 and growing (Ken Research). Analysts describe budgets moving to performance marketing, short video, search, social commerce and retail media. These are qualitative statements, not measured shares.
- **TV is no longer the default, but not dead.** The last public split found is MAGNA 2022: linear 62% of ad budgets, TV 58% and falling. CTV (YouTube on TV, FPT Play, VTV Go, MyTV) is sold as reaching 30M+ monthly viewers (seller claim). The 2026 World Cup lifted TV-set demand.
- **Unit costs (rough, practitioner estimates):** TikTok CPM about VND 25–50K vs Facebook about VND 50–150K for the same vertical; CPC about VND 3–5K, plus 5% VAT on top-ups. These figures are indicative only.
- **Platform economics are shifting toward marketplaces and messaging.** Fee increases on Shopee and TikTok Shop (H1 2026) and tax withholding on marketplace sellers (from 1 Jul 2025) raise the cost of selling on platforms. Meta's 2026 message for Vietnam is "conversational business" (ads → chat → purchase). YouTube's pitch is trust (82% more likely to buy on a trusted creator's recommendation) and a claimed 1.5× ROI vs all-media average (2022–2025, Google's own number).
